🔑 Key Takeaways from Phase 1
LangChain = provider-agnostic LLM interface. Swap Groq ↔ OpenAI ↔ Ollama with one line.

Messages are typed and role-based. System sets behavior; Human/AI form the dialogue.

Three call styles: invoke (block), stream (tokens), batch (parallel).

No built-in memory. You pass the entire history every call.

Token usage = cost driver. Check usage_metadata to estimate.
